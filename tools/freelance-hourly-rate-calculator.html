How it works

The calculator works backwards from what you want to keep. It grosses your take-home pay up for tax, adds business costs and a safety margin, then divides by the hours you can actually bill once time off and admin are taken out. The full method is in our guide, How to Calculate Your Freelance Hourly Rate.

Use your own tax share: it depends on where you live and how much you earn. This is an estimate, not tax advice.
